How We Play

Community Language Note

Our core in-game chat, official announcements, and Discord voice channels are predominantly conducted in Lithuanian. However, many of our members speak fluent English, and we warmly accommodate international players who value a relaxed, cooperative, and supportive home.

We Leave No One Behind

Support through real actions, not hollow words. We provide starter bags, guide newcomers through early hurdles, and venture into dungeons together. No one walks alone.

Recreation, Not a Second Job

We all have personal lives, careers, and families. Want to explore the world peacefully at your own pace? Please do. Want to raid in the evenings? We have an organized schedule, with zero forced pressure.

Open and Transparent

No backroom deals or favoritism. All guild decisions, the guild bank, and loot distribution are open, transparent, and fair from day one.

Guild Ranks

We do not divide players into "good" and "bad". Our ranks simply reflect your playstyle and the time you wish to dedicate to the game. Choose the path that fits you best:

Camp Tips

Gold Doesn't Grow on Trees

Only train the spells and ranks you actively cast in battle. Otherwise, at level 40 you'll be looking wistfully at others riding mounts while you still run on foot.

Synergistic Professions

We recommend choosing complementary professions (e.g., Herbalism and Alchemy or Mining and Engineering) for immediate personal and team utility.

Dungeon Etiquette

Always let the tank strike first so the healer doesn't go gray. Only roll "Need" on gear you can equip immediately for your active spec; otherwise, politely roll "Greed".

Loot Rules

Fair Loot Distribution (SR+1)

In raids, we use the simple Soft-Reserve system. Before each raid, you select the one item you want most. When that item drops, only players who reserved it roll for it. Winning awards a +1 mark, giving subsequent priority to teammates who haven't won loot yet. Exceptions apply only to the Main Tank and core Healers, whose survivability and mana ensure the entire team stays alive.
