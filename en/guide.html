Beginner's Game Guide

What is World of Warcraft?

World of Warcraft (WoW) is one of the world's most legendary Massively Multiplayer Online Role-Playing Games (MMORPG). Players create their own fantasy avatars, explore the vast living world of Azeroth, embark on epic questlines, battle fearsome monsters, and forge friendships with fellow adventurers.

The world is split between two rival factions: the Alliance and the Horde. You can choose from diverse races (Humans, Dwarves, Gnomes, Night Elves, etc.) and iconic classes (Warriors, Mages, Priests, etc.). While you can wander solo, the game truly shines when playing together with friendly companions — organizing into guilds to conquer challenging encounters as one.

Game Content and Player Roles

World of Warcraft offers a rich spectrum of adventures — from relaxed open-world questing to intense coordinated group battles. Core group content includes:

  • Dungeons: Designed for 5-player parties. Requires solid teamwork to defeat elite enemies and challenging bosses.
  • Raids: The pinnacle of PvE challenges, built for 20 or 40-player armies. Here lie Azeroth's greatest perils and most coveted treasures.
  • PvP (Player vs Player): Skirmishes between Alliance and Horde heroes in structured Battlegrounds or the open world.

In group content, each player fulfills one of three essential roles:

  • Tank: The vanguard shield. The tank's job is holding monster threat (aggro) so foes strike them rather than fragile teammates. Tanks wear the thickest armor.
  • Healer: The party's lifeblood. Their duty is monitoring health bars (especially the tank's), replenishing lost hit points, dispelling debuffs, and reviving fallen allies.
  • Damage Dealer (DPS): The offensive force. Their task is eliminating threats swiftly (in melee or from distance) while the tank maintains control and the healer restores health.

Dungeon Etiquette (5-Player Party Rules):

  • Let the tank pull: Never attack unengaged monsters first — wait for the tank to initiate and establish threat.
  • Watch the healer's mana: Before engaging the next pack, make sure your healer has sufficient mana (the blue bar).
  • Need vs. Greed: Only roll Need on items that are an immediate upgrade for your current main role. Roll Greed on items intended for selling or off-spec.
Quests and World Exploration

Most experience points (XP) in Classic WoW are earned by completing quests. Here are the core conventions to remember:

  • Exclamation Mark (!): A yellow exclamation point over an NPC means an available quest for your level. A gray exclamation point indicates your character is not yet high enough level.
  • Question Mark (?): A yellow question mark means the quest objectives are complete and you can claim your reward. A gray question mark means you still have unfinished objectives.
  • Read the quest text: Classic WoW does not show map pins, glowing arrows, or automated GPS trail markers. Quest dialogs tell you exactly where to head (e.g. "head east along the riverbank past the mill") and what to search for.

What to do if you run out of quests?
In Classic WoW, quests do not follow a single linear railroad. If you run out of quests or they become too high level (orange/red):

  • Travel to other racial zones: The Alliance has multiple zones for every level range. Humans can take the Deeprun Tram to Dwarf lands to explore Loch Modan or Darkshore.
  • Check taverns and watchposts: Explore every crossroad and settlement for yellow exclamation marks (!).
  • Run 5-man dungeons: Dungeons provide huge bursts of quest experience and great gear upgrades.
  • Ask in guild chat: Just ask in /g — guildmates will gladly suggest the best leveling zone for your current level.
Professions: Earning Gold and Thriving

Professions allow you to gather raw materials, craft powerful gear, and build a steady income. They are divided into two categories: Primary (you can choose up to 2) and Secondary (you can learn all 3).

1. Primary Profession Synergies (Choose up to 2):
Every crafting profession naturally pairs with a specific gathering profession so you don't have to buy expensive materials on the Auction House:

Profession Synergy (Pair) Best Suited For How It Works & Key Benefits
Herbalism + Alchemy All Classes Gather wild herbs to brew health/mana potions, stat elixirs, and endgame flasks. Indispensable and consistently profitable for level 60 raiding.
Mining + Blacksmithing Warriors, Paladins Mine ore veins to forge heavy Mail and Plate armor, shields, weapons, and weapon sharpening stones.
Mining + Engineering All Classes
(especially PvP & Tanks)		 Turn ore and metal into bombs, stun grenades, AoE explosives, scopes, and unique gadgets. Offers the highest utility in the game.
Skinning + Leatherworking Rogues, Druids, Hunters, Shamans Skin defeated beasts to craft Leather and Mail armor, quivers, and armor reinforcement kits.
Tailoring + Enchanting Mages, Warlocks, Priests Requires no gathering skill! Weave Cloth dropped by humanoids into bags and robes, then Disenchant crafted gear into magical dust to permanently enchant equipment.
Skinning + Mining / Herbalism
(Dual Gathering)		 Beginners & Gold Savers Two gathering skills. Sell all collected hides, ores, and herbs on the Auction House — the fastest and easiest way to save 100 gold for your level 40 mount!

2. Secondary Professions (Learn all 3!):
These never count toward your 2-profession limit, so every adventurer can and should master all three:

Secondary Profession Requirements & Materials What It Is Good For & Benefits
First Aid Cloth drops (Linen, Wool, Silk, Mageweave, Runecloth) looted from humanoid enemies. Mandatory for every class! Turns spare cloth into bandages that rapidly restore health in or out of combat without spending mana or buying food. Essential for Warriors, Rogues, Hunters, and Warlocks.
Cooking Meat from beasts or fish caught via Fishing, basic spices, and a campfire or stove. Cooked meals quickly recover health between fights and grant long-lasting "Well Fed" stat buffs (extra Stamina, Spirit, or Attack Power) that are invaluable in dungeons and raids.
Fishing A Fishing Pole, optional lures (Shiny Baubles), and any body of water. Directly fuels Cooking (free food supply) and Alchemy (alchemists pay top gold for Oily Blackmouth, Firefin Snapper, and Stonescale Eel for raid potions). A relaxing, steady gold-maker!
Death in Azeroth: What Happens?

Death is never permanent in World of Warcraft, but it carries a tangible consequence:

  • The Ghost Run: When you perish, your spirit awakens at the nearest graveyard as a ghost. Run back to your body and click "Resurrect" to return to life.
  • Durability Degradation: Every death deducts 10% durability from equipped armor and weapons. When durability hits 0%, an item loses all benefits until repaired at a blacksmith or armorer for gold.
  • The Spirit Healer: If you die in an unreachable abyss, the graveyard angel can resurrect you on the spot. Warning: This causes an additional 25% durability penalty to your entire inventory and inflicts a 10-minute "Resurrection Sickness" that severely reduces your stats. Use only as a last resort!
Economy: Copper, Silver, and Gold

The monetary system is straightforward, but currency is scarce in early levels. Here is how it functions:

  • Currency Tiers: 100 Copper = 1 Silver. 100 Silver = 1 Gold.
  • Vendor Junk: Sell all gray (Poor) items to any merchant immediately. They exist purely to generate pocket change.
  • Auction House: Green (Uncommon) and Blue (Rare) items or gathered trade materials (ores, herbs, cloth) are best sold at the Auction House in major Alliance cities (such as Stormwind or Ironforge), where other players will pay far more than generic vendors.
  • Save for Your Mount: At level 40, your first mount and riding training cost nearly 100 gold. Only purchase the spell ranks you actively use and conserve your wealth!
All 9 Alliance Classes and Their Roles

In Classic World of Warcraft, player roles fall into three core specializations: Tanks (shielding allies), Healers (preserving life), and Damage Dealers / DPS (destroying foes). On our realm, the Alliance commands 9 distinct classes:

Warrior

Tank / Melee DPS • Plate Armor

The ultimate vanguard fighter and premier raid tank. Dons the heaviest plate armor, commands enemy threat, or unleashes devastating melee physical damage. Empowers the party with vital Battle Shout attack power and delivers deadly weapon strikes.

Paladin

Healer / Tank / Melee DPS • Blessings & Auras

Holy champion of the Alliance and an unyielding pillar on the battlefield. Features exceptionally efficient single-target healing, wears heavy plate armor, and relies on invulnerability shields (Divine Shield). Bestows indispensable party Blessings, defensive Auras, and resurrects fallen comrades.

Priest

Healer / Ranged Magic • Holy Shields & Shadow

The most versatile and essential raid healer in Azeroth. Wears cloth armor, shielding allies with sacred absorption barriers or flaying enemy minds with shadow magic. Bolsters the entire group's health pool with the iconic Power Word: Fortitude stamina buff.

Shaman

Healer / Hybrid DPS • Totemic Power & Elements

Master of primal elements, blending natural forces with physical martial prowess. Wears mail armor, heals multiple allies simultaneously with Chain Heal, or strikes foes with lightning and fire. Plants elemental totems that grant massive melee attack speed, mana regeneration, and elemental resistances.

Mage

Ranged Magic (DPS) • Crowd Control & Portals

Master of arcane, flame, and frost destructive spellcasting. Wears cloth armor, decimating enemy packs with unparalleled area-of-effect (AoE) damage while locking down encounters with freezes and Polymorph. Provides the party with complimentary food, conjured water, intellect buffs, and convenient teleportation portals.

Warlock

Ranged Magic (DPS) • Curses, Demons & Summons

Practitioner of the dark arts who binds demonic minions to his will. Wears cloth armor, slowly draining enemy vitality through debilitating damage-over-time curses and shadow magic. Supplies allies with healing Healthstones, wards off wipes with Soulstones, and summons absent raid members across Azeroth.

Rogue

Melee DPS • Stealth, Traps & Poisons

Lethal assassin of the shadows, striking silently and swiftly from behind. Wears leather armor, coats dual weapons with potent poisons, and unleashes massive burst damage from stealth. Disables priority targets, reliably interrupts spellcasters (Kick), and unlocks trapped dungeon mechanisms and lockboxes.

Hunter

Ranged Physical (DPS) • Beasts, Traps & Survival

Wilderness marksman fighting in tandem with a loyal bonded pet. Wears mail armor, attacks from range with bows or guns, and controls the battlefield through utility traps. Safely pulls dangerous monsters to the tank and provides an attack power aura for ranged squad members.

Druid

Versatile Hybrid • Tank, Healer, or Damage Dealer

Guardian of nature capable of shapeshifting instantly to fit any encounter. Wears leather armor and flexibly serves as a sturdy bear tank, a swift cat predator, or a healing druid. Provides party-wide Mark of the Wild blessings, emergency mana restoration (Innervate), and in-combat resurrection.
