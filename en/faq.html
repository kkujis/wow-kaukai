Frequently Asked Questions

What is the primary language spoken in the guild?

Our guild is predominantly Lithuanian-speaking. Our in-game guild chat, official Discord announcements, and Discord voice channels are conducted primarily in Lithuanian.

Are English speakers welcome? Absolutely yes! Many of our officers and members speak fluent English. We are glad to translate key raid tactics, answer gameplay questions, and accommodate international adventurers who appreciate our calm, unhurried, and drama-free community.

What realm and faction do you play on?

We play on the WoW Forever realm. We represent the Alliance faction. Importantly, we play on a PvE (Player vs Environment) realm, meaning open-world PvP combat only happens when you voluntarily enable your PvP flag.

Do you accept complete beginners? What advice do you have for newcomers?

Absolutely yes! Newcomers are warmly welcomed. Classic WoW can seem challenging at first, so here are our key recommendations for your early journey:

  • Don't rush. The game is designed to be enjoyed at a steady pace, not rushed to the level cap. Read quest lore, explore the world, and soak in the adventure.
  • Ask questions. If you don't understand something — ask in guild chat or on Discord. There will always be someone happy to explain and help out.
  • Save your gold. Money is scarce early on. Don't train every single ability rank from trainers — only buy the spells you actually cast in combat. You will need nearly 100 gold to purchase your mount at level 40!
How does the raid loot distribution system (SR+1) work?

In raids, we don't use complex point systems (DKP) or chaotic free-for-all rolling. We employ Soft-Reserve +1 (SR+1) — a transparent, community-minded, and fair framework that prevents scenarios where one lucky player takes everything while others leave empty-handed:

  • 1. Pre-Raid Reservation (Soft-Reserve): Before each run, every raid participant locks in 1 desired item via a dedicated link (shared in our Discord raid channel) from that raid's boss loot table.
  • 2. Rolling for Reserved Loot: When a boss is defeated and that item drops, only the players who reserved it roll the dice (/roll 100). If you were the sole person to reserve it — it is awarded to you guaranteed without contest!
  • 3. The "+1" Rule (Fairness Safeguard): Every main item won adds a "+1" mark for that raid lockout. If another item you can use drops later, priority goes to teammates who haven't won anything yet in that run (0 wins have priority over +1, and +1 over +2). This ensures loot is distributed evenly across the whole team.
  • 4. Unreserved Items (MS > OS): If an item drops that no one reserved, all eligible players may roll if it matches their primary spec (Main Spec, taking existing "+1" marks into account). If no one needs it for their main spec, it opens to secondary roles (Off-Spec).
  • 5. Core Priority Exceptions (Main Tank & Healers): Priority exceptions may be granted to the Main Tank and core Healers for vital survivability or critical mana items, as a solid tank and sustained healers guarantee the entire raid's survival.
Is Discord required?

Discord is our primary communication and announcement hub. While speaking on microphone is never mandatory, all official raid sign-ups, guild updates, and useful guides are posted there, so joining is strongly recommended.
